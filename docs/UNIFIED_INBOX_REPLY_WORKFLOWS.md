# Unified inbox reply workflows

The workspace-scoped unified inbox groups inbound email webhook messages, native Meta WhatsApp inbound messages, and outbound email/WhatsApp delivery records by CRM prospect. Thread previews and chronological history include direction, channel, provider and outbound delivery state. Failed or uncertain outbound attempts remain visible as delivery activity rather than being presented as successful sends.

Workspace members can create a reply draft from the selected thread. The endpoint validates workspace membership, channel contact details, email suppression or documented WhatsApp opt-in and opt-out state, requires a current HTTPS source-evidence URL and an active campaign, and writes the draft to the existing pending-approval queue with a draft event. It never sends directly. Email replies continue through the existing SMTP delivery queue after approval; WhatsApp replies continue through the approved-template, account, consent, policy and quota-gated queue. Duplicate pending drafts are prevented by the existing database uniqueness constraint.

Message history is ordered chronologically and paginated. The inbox remains dependent on inbound email webhook and Meta webhook processor configuration. Outbound history is sourced from durable delivery records; provider dispatch and delivery statuses remain subject to live credentials, provider activation and worker operation.
